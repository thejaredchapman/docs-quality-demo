# Protect a streak with a streak freeze

This page is for people who want to take a day off without losing a long streak.

A streak freeze keeps your streak going for one day when you don't check off a habit. You get two freezes each month.

## Use a streak freeze

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Streak freeze**.
3. Choose the habit you want to protect.
4. Just choose the day.
