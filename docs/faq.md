# Frequently asked questions

This page answers common questions from Tally users.

## Is Tally free?

Yes. The habit tracker is free. **Cloud sync** is part of Tally Plus.

## How do I sign in with Google?

Tally supports OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, so tap **Continue with Google** on the sign-in screen.

## Can I use Tally offline?

Yes. Your check-ins are saved on your device and sync when you're back online.
